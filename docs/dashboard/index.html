BRACE evaluation

When should you be at the stop to catch your bus 9 times out of 10? Historical evaluation on recorded Zurich city bus (VBZ) days. Every number here is what a rider would have experienced on those days, computed from measured departures.

Waiting time against missed buses

Each method can aim for any miss rate: aim lower and you wait longer. The offset for each aim was set on validation days, then measured on the test days. Lower and further left is better. The dashed line is the 10% aim the project was built around.
